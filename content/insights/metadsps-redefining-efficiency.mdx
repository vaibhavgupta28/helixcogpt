## Summary
MetaDSP platforms fuse fragmented DSP workflows into a shared control plane, turning human strategy and AI feedback loops into compounding performance gains.

## What’s Changed
- Multi-DSP buyers now manage $2B+ in annual media with dozens of market-specific guardrails.
- Signal loss is pressuring audience quality, forcing precision orchestration across 50B+ daily impressions.
- AI models have matured to ingest petabyte-scale telemetry and return actionable guidance in near real time.

## Architecture Patterns
- Normalize pacing, budget, and deal constructs through API adapters that abstract vendor nuance.
- Operate an experimentation fabric with automated QA, treatment allocation, and rollback automation.
- Expose observability primitives—latency, cost curves, creative health—to traders through a single UI.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Automation decisions violate policy. **Mitigation:** Embed rule engines and human approval queues.
- **Risk:** Signal fragmentation lowers model accuracy. **Mitigation:** Invest in enrichment and consent-aware identity stitching.
- **Risk:** Change management lags platform upgrades. **Mitigation:** Ship runbooks, training modules, and co-piloting sessions.

## Key Takeaways
MetaDSP adoption requires a balance of automation and human oversight, but the payoff is waste reductions of 30% or more with faster optimization loops.
