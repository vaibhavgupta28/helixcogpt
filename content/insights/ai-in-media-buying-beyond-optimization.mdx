## Summary
AI in media buying has expanded from bid optimization to decision orchestration, touching planning, activation, and measurement simultaneously.

## What’s Changed
- Teams expect AI agents to flag anomalies, propose experiments, and manage governance escalations.
- Trading desks are restructuring runbooks to consume AI insights alongside human judgment.
- Vendors expose more granular APIs, giving operators the hooks needed to embed bespoke logic.

## Architecture Patterns
- Deploy AI copilots that translate telemetry into recommended playbooks with approval workflows.
- Instrument closed-loop experimentation: design, launch, measure, and iterate with automated QA.
- Maintain feature stores that balance real-time signals with durable historical context.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Over-reliance on opaque models. **Mitigation:** Pair models with interpretable metrics and rollback triggers.
- **Risk:** Governance gaps when AI acts autonomously. **Mitigation:** Enforce human-in-the-loop approvals for critical changes.
- **Risk:** Fragmented data pipelines. **Mitigation:** Centralize signal management with schema registries and validation suites.

## Key Takeaways
AI now drives workflow and decision velocity. Teams that orchestrate AI with human oversight unlock structural advantages.
