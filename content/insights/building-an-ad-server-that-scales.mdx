## Summary
Modern ad servers must execute trillions of decisions with deterministic latency targets while honoring policy, privacy, and monetization goals.

## What’s Changed
- Header bidding and server-to-server demand create unpredictable traffic profiles.
- Privacy regulations require contextual fallbacks when user-level identifiers are limited.
- Observability expectations have risen: operators need streaming visibility to catch anomalies instantly.

## Architecture Patterns
- Use geo-aware routing with edge decisioning backed by centralized policy management.
- Separate bidding, decisioning, and logging planes to scale independently and isolate failures.
- Instrument streaming analytics for pacing, discrepancy detection, and real-time experiment feedback.

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Latency regressions during traffic spikes. **Mitigation:** Autoscale at the edge with performance SLO alarms.
- **Risk:** Policy drift across markets. **Mitigation:** Maintain policy registries with automated integration tests.
- **Risk:** Logging gaps create blind spots. **Mitigation:** Mirror logs into redundant stores with schema validation.

## Key Takeaways
Ad servers that couple edge intelligence with centralized governance maintain reliability while adapting to market shifts.
