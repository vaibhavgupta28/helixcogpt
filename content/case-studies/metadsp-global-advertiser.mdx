## Problem
A global retail advertiser managed spend across four DSPs with inconsistent pacing, misaligned budgets, and siloed reporting. Waste levels were 30% above target, experimentation was stalled, and compliance reviews spanned multiple time zones.

## Approach
Helix led a diagnostics sprint to map signal integrity, bidding guardrails, and workflow friction. The team designed a MetaDSP control plane that normalized inventory taxonomies, activated identity resolution, and introduced governance hooks for each market.

## Architecture Highlights
- Unified trafficking adapter orchestrating four DSP APIs with deterministic pacing logic.
- Real-time signal enrichment pipeline harmonizing audience, site, and conversion feeds with privacy-first policies.
- Experimentation harness enabling parallel testing, automated QA, and audit-ready logging.

## Results
- 30% reduction in media waste within the first six weeks of deployment.
- Consolidated reporting surfaces delivering a single source of truth across trading teams.
- Optimization cycles compressed from weekly to daily, with rollbacks automated by policy.

## Stack
- MetaDSP control plane built on TypeScript, Next.js, and serverless orchestration.
- Streaming enrichment via Apache Flink, Snowflake, and dbt transformations.
- Observability powered by OpenTelemetry, Grafana, and incident automation runbooks.
