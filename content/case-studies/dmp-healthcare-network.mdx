## Problem
A healthcare network needed a white-label DMP to activate provider, consent, and outcomes data across strict privacy boundaries. Legacy enrichment jobs were brittle, and activation to downstream channels took weeks.

## Approach
Helix delivered a privacy-first architecture leveraging consent-aware identity graphs, HIPAA-compliant data stores, and zero-trust access controls. We built deterministic and probabilistic models to surface high-value cohorts without exposing PHI.

## Architecture Highlights
- Consent-aware identity graph blending clinical, CRM, and media signals with governance automation.
- Differential privacy layer enforcing data minimization and tokenization before activation.
- Workflow automation connecting segment design, QA, and push-to-channel integrations.

## Results
- 10× improvement in audience precision while adhering to regional privacy requirements.
- Time-to-activation dropped from 21 days to under 48 hours with automated QA.
- Privacy office gained real-time oversight via dashboards and immutable audit trails.

## Stack
- Data platform built on Snowflake, Apache Iceberg, and dbt for governed transformations.
- Identity resolution powered by custom graph services, AWS Neptune, and Python microservices.
- Activation connectors orchestrated through serverless functions with automated monitoring.
